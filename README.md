## 👋 About

A Saudi AI engineer who wants to build what comes next in AI and make it work for people here.

I'm a Software Engineering senior at **Umm Al-Qura University**. I build **LLM agents, RAG systems and evaluation pipelines**, ship them as full-stack products, and research how to apply AI to real problems.

**Now**
- 🔬 Research on Arabic LLM evaluation and vision-language models
- 🛠️ Building agentic tooling at MawHub and the Sourcing Harness
- 🧪 Designing benchmarks that show where models fail

**Focus areas:** LLM agents · RAG · LLM evaluation · Fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA) · Arabic NLP · Full-stack (Python, Django, FastAPI, React, AWS)
